Size & fit guide

Measure once, wear comfortably

Use a soft tape measure (or a piece of string and a ruler). Measure snugly but not tight, and send us the number in inches.

Care & longevity

Keep your beadwork brilliant

Beaded bags

  • Stuff lightly with tissue paper to hold the shape in storage.
  • Store upright in a dust bag, away from direct sunlight.
  • Avoid overfilling—stick to everyday essentials.
  • Wipe the surface with a dry, soft cloth.

Pearls & crystals

  • Put jewellery on last—after perfume, lotion and hairspray.
  • Wipe gently with a soft cloth after wearing.
  • Store necklaces flat and separately to avoid scratches.
  • Keep away from harsh cleaning chemicals.

Waistbeads

  • Traditional tie-on: tie with a secure double knot and trim the ends; designed to stay on.
  • Clasp styles: fasten the screw or lobster clasp fully and remove before swimming or sleeping if preferred.
  • Rinse gently and pat dry after contact with sweat or salt water.

Bracelets & anklets

  • Roll stretch bracelets on and off—don’t pull to stretch them.
  • Anklets are water-resistant; dry them after the beach or pool.
  • Store in a pouch to keep stacks tangle-free.
